The Vision & Approach

First-Principles Residential Development in Northern Virginia

Located in the established Dunn Loring Woods neighborhood of Vienna—just minutes from the Orange Line Metro and the W&OD Trail—2531 Villanova Dr. represents a disciplined approach to residential construction and land stewardship.

Led by Cody Woodard, a Virginia Class A Licensed Residential Contractor with deep roots in horizontal land development and civil infrastructure, this project prioritizes long-term structural integrity, sub-surface drainage engineering, and clean architectural execution over superficial cosmetic flips.

Construction Milestones

The Build Journey

A transparent log of pre-construction, civil site work, utility infrastructure, and structural phases.

Completed PHASE 01 • Q4 2025

Land Acquisition & Due Diligence

Acquired the ~10,000 sq. ft. non-HOA parcel in Dunn Loring Woods. Conducted comprehensive boundary surveys, soil evaluations, tree canopy assessments, and Fairfax County zoning feasibility studies for long-term property optimization.

Completed PHASE 02 • Q1–Q2 2026

Licensing, Permitting & Pre-Construction

Formalized Woodard Construction LLC operations and secured Virginia Class A Residential Contractor credentials. Finalized civil grading plans, structural engineering drawings, and trade partner scopes of work.

Completed PHASE 03 • Q3 2026

Underground Utility Conversion & Trenching

Eliminated overhead visual clutter and weather vulnerability by trenching and converting overhead electrical and communication service laterals into dedicated underground PVC conduit runs coordinated with local utility providers.

In Progress PHASE 04 • Q4 2026

Foundation Drainage, Grading & Interior Build-Out

Executing perimeter foundation drainage upgrades, downspout piping extensions, positive swale grading, and precision interior rough-ins—including high-capacity electrical infrastructure ready for Level 2 EV charging.

Upcoming PHASE 05 • 2027

Architectural Finishes, Hardscaping & Final Turf

Final exterior cladding details, custom millwork, permeable hardscape walkways, and full-lot topsoil stabilization and turf establishment.

Under the Hood

Infrastructure & Build Standards

Great residential construction starts below grade. Every decision on 2531 Villanova Dr. is guided by civil engineering discipline.

Sub-Surface Utilities

Full conversion of aerial electrical and fiber/telecom drops to buried conduit, protecting service reliability against Northern Virginia storms and improving curb appeal.

Water Management & Grading

Engineered positive slope away from all foundation walls, paired with buried rigid PVC drain lines and cleanouts to keep basements bone-dry year-round.

Future-Ready Systems

Upgraded electrical service panels with dedicated 240V circuits for Level 2 EV charging, high-efficiency HVAC zoning, and structured low-voltage wiring throughout.
