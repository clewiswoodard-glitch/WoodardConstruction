MANIFESTO // FREEDOM OF INFORMATION & EDUCATION

WHY I AM PUBLISHING THE BLUEPRINT, THE NUMBERS, AND THE MISTAKES

Residential construction and real estate development have historically operated behind a curtain of gatekept pricing, vague contractor estimates, and "trade secrets." If you are a homeowner trying to understand why a utility trench costs what it does, or an aspiring small-scale builder trying to pull your first permit in Fairfax County, reliable ground-truth data is almost impossible to find.

I built this site for one reason: freedom of information and practical education.

By documenting every phase of 2531 Villanova Dr. in public—including real budget line items, municipal permitting hurdles, civil engineering details, and field photos—I want to create an open-source reference manual that anyone can study, audit, and learn from.
